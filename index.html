Questions

The things you'd ask first.

Are you doing the legal work?

No. Estate Desk is administrative support — data-collation, document preparation and tracking. Your firm remains instructed, reviews everything, and applies for the grant. Applying for a grant of probate for reward is a reserved legal activity under the Legal Services Act 2007, and that stays entirely with you.

What happens to the estate's data?

Data stays in the UK, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is used only for the matter you send — never shared, never used to train anything. A DPA and NDA are available on request before you send a single file. When a matter closes, the data is returned or deleted on your instruction. And estate funds are never touched — this is a software-and-admin layer behind your firm.

Is the first case really free?

Yes. The best way to judge the time saved is to see it on one live file. I'll run the first matter at no charge; after that it's a flat fee per matter, with no lock-in.

Do we have to change our case-management system?

No. Estate Desk slots alongside whatever you use today — Clio, LEAP, Actionstep or a spreadsheet. The output is a clean pack your fee-earner reviews. No migration.
